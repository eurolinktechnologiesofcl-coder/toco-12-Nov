Privacy Policy

Last updated: January 2026. This policy outlines how TOCO Technologies collects, uses, and safeguards personal and corporate data in compliance with UAE Federal Decree-Law No. 45 of 2021.

1. Information We Collect

When you submit an enquiry through our website, communicate via WhatsApp, or engage our engineering services, we collect contact information including your full name, corporate email, UAE phone number, company name, and project scope.

2. Legal Basis for Processing

We process your data strictly under the provisions of UAE Federal Decree-Law No. 45 of 2021 on Personal Data Protection to provide engineering proposals, fulfill contractual deliverables, and comply with statutory financial audits.

3. Data Sovereignty & Security

We implement bank-grade encryption (AES-256 at rest, TLS 1.3 in transit). Client repositories and production databases are deployed in ISO 27001-certified data centers or local UAE AWS cloud regions to preserve national data sovereignty.

4. Third-Party Sharing

TOCO Technologies never sells, rents, or monetizes client data to third-party advertisers. Data is shared exclusively with designated infrastructure providers (e.g. AWS UAE, official WhatsApp Cloud API) required to operate your services.

5. Your Rights Under UAE Law

You retain the right to access, rectify, or request erasure of your personal data at any time by contacting our Data Protection Officer at info@tocoplus.com.
