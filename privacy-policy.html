Privacy Policy

Your privacy is important to us. Learn how TOCO Technologies collects, uses, and safeguards your information.

1. Information We Collect

We collect information that you provide directly to us when contacting us, requesting sales consultations, or working with us. This may include your name, business email address, phone number, company details, and project requirements.

2. How We Use Your Information

We use the collected information to:

  • Provide, maintain, and improve our digital, web, mobile, and software engineering services.
  • Respond to your comments, technical questions, quotation enquiries, and support requests.
  • Communicate with you about project updates, technical proposals, and milestone deliveries.
  • Monitor and analyze website performance to optimize user experience and security.

3. Information Sharing and Disclosure

We do not share, sell, or rent your personal information to third parties. We may disclose information only if required by law or in good faith belief that such action is necessary to comply with applicable UAE legal obligations.

4. Data Security

We implement industry-standard administrative, technical, and physical security measures to protect your personal and business information from unauthorized access, alteration, disclosure, or destruction.

5. Your Rights and Choices

Depending on your location, you may have the right to access, update, correct, or request deletion of your personal data. To exercise these rights, please contact us at info@tocoplus.com or call our Dubai office on +971 4 577 0725.

6. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Any material updates will be posted on this page. Last updated: November 2026.
