Comfity — Automated UAE Payroll & WPS Compliance

Say goodbye to payroll calculation errors and late WPS filing penalties. Comfity is our proprietary payroll automation platform engineered specifically for UAE labor law, Ministry of Human Resources and Emiratisation (MOHRE) compliance, and one-click SIF file generation.

Platform Capabilities

Engineered for High-Velocity Business Execution

Every feature is designed to reduce friction, eliminate operational error, and accelerate commercial workflow.

💼

One-Click UAE WPS SIF File Generation

Automatically format and validate Wages Protection System (WPS) .SIF files for instant processing across all UAE banks and exchange houses.

⚖️

UAE Labor Law & MOHRE Gratuity Engine

Automated end-of-service gratuity calculations compliant with the latest UAE Federal Decree-Law No. 33 on Labor Relations.

📅

Automated Leave & Attendance Tracking

Track annual leaves, sick leaves, public UAE holidays, and unpaid leave with automatic monthly salary deductions and accruals.

📑

Confidential Digital Payslip Generation

Automated PDF payslip distribution directly to employee emails or private self-service employee mobile portals.

🇦🇪

Emiratisation (GPSSA) Pension Support

Built-in calculations for UAE national employee contributions to the General Pension and Social Security Authority (GPSSA).

🔒

Bank-Grade Encryption & Audit Trails

Encrypted employee salary records, role-based HR access control, and complete historical audit logs.
