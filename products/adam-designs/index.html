Adam Designs — Interior Quote & Sales Automation Platform

Architected specifically for interior design studios, fit-out contractors, and architectural firms in Dubai and Abu Dhabi. Adam Designs automates complex bill-of-quantities (BOQ), material estimation, and client proposal generation in minutes.

Request Platform Demo WhatsApp Product Specialist
Platform Capabilities

Engineered for High-Velocity Business Execution

Every feature is designed to reduce friction, eliminate operational error, and accelerate commercial workflow.

📐

Automated Room & Space Dimension Calculator

Input space dimensions, wall treatments, flooring specs, and ceiling heights to automatically compute exact material volumes.

💰

Dynamic Material & Labor Pricing Engine

Real-time cost indexing for UAE marbles, joinery, lighting, plumbing, and contractor labor rates with custom profit margins.

📑

Instant Luxury Client Proposals in PDF

Generate breathtaking, branded client quotation decks with 3D moodboard attachments, itemized pricing, and terms in one click.

✍️

Digital E-Signature & Milestone Payments

Clients review, configure finish options, sign contracts electronically, and pay initial mobilization deposits via secure online checkout.

📊

Sales Pipeline & Estimator Performance Tracking

Comprehensive management dashboard tracking win rates, pending quotes, estimator velocity, and average project deal size.

🔄

Direct ERP & Procurement Sync

Approved quotes automatically generate purchase orders, supplier material requests, and project dispatch schedules.
